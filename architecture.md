# Comprehension Stack Architecture

This is a conceptual architecture for human and institutional sensemaking. The layers describe intended responsibilities and relationships; their arrangement does not establish a tested integration or a universal behavioral guarantee.

## Layers and boundaries

| Layer | Intended contribution | What must remain separate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human sensemaking | Comprehension Scope helps people examine claims, incentives and uncertainty. | A helpful interpretation is not evidence of factual truth or permission. |
| Shared evidence | Cognous Evidence Registry records claim/evidence commitments and lifecycle history. | Registration, evidentiary support and independent corroboration are different properties. |
| Attestation | Cognous Evidence Attestation checks issuer signatures under configured trust assumptions. | A verified signature does not establish the signed proposition's truth or legitimate authority. |
| Interaction guidance | Truth–Freedom–Agency provides reasoning and interaction guidance. | Written instructions are distinct from implementation, testing and measured efficacy. |
| Institutional governance | Alvorada addresses authority, proportional process, challenge and correction. | A proposed framework requires competent adoption; neither a repository nor an agent confers that adoption. |
| Bounded execution | Cognous Execution Runtime participates in the runtime chain described by the hub. | Authorization, execution acknowledgement, destination observation and independent verification remain separate. |

Current repository links are maintained in the [project map](README.md#project-responsibilities). Components remain independently usable; this map does not make optional reasoning or sensemaking projects prerequisites for the selected runtime workflow.

## Design goals

The original architecture identified neutrality, agency preservation, interpretive minimalism, modularity, universality and adversarial robustness as invariants. Here they are explicitly **design goals**, not properties proven for every implementation or environment.

- Examine assumptions and perspectives rather than claim the absence of all embedded values.
- Preserve informed human agency, including legitimate review and correction, while respecting justified constraints.
- Provide structure and uncertainty rather than manufacture conclusions.
- Keep component responsibilities and replacement boundaries explicit.
- Treat cross-cultural applicability and resistance to manipulation as questions requiring evidence in the relevant setting.
